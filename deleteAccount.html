Delete Your DPI GO Account

You can permanently delete your account in the DPI GO app. Once the deletion is complete, your account and its associated data cannot be recovered.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the DPI GO app, go to the login page, and sign in with your account name and password.
  2. Open the Mine tab, then tap the Settings icon to go to the Settings page.
  3. Open Account & Security, then tap Apply to cancel the account.
  4. Follow the on-screen instructions to confirm the account deletion.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Account and profile information
  • Vehicle binding information
  • Published posts and related information
  • All other data associated with the account
Data Retention Period

When the account is deleted, all data listed above will be permanently deleted and will not be retained.
