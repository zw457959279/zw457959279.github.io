I. How We Collect and Use Personal Information
(1) Principles for Collecting and Using Personal Information
We follow the principles of lawfulness, legitimacy and necessity, and collect only the necessary information related to your use of the product and services. We will not collect personal information in a forcibly bundled manner merely because you have consented to this Privacy Policy. When you use our products and services, we need/may need to collect and use your personal information. Through this Privacy Policy, we will inform you of the purpose, manner and scope of collecting and using your personal information, as well as the impact of your refusal to provide the corresponding information. If you refuse to provide non-essential information, it will not affect your use of the basic functions of this Service.
(2) Scenarios for Collecting and Using Personal Information
1. Account Registration and Login
(1) When you register or log in to an account, in accordance with the requirements of relevant laws and regulations, you need to provide us with your mobile phone number as information to identify you, and we will verify your identity by sending an SMS verification code. If you do not provide your mobile phone number, you will be unable to create an account or use this Service.
(2) You may also use the "third-party login" feature to log in with accounts of third-party platforms (such as WeChat and QQ). You will authorize us to obtain your information on the third-party platform (such as nickname and avatar) for binding with your Guoqi Dayou Space-Time account. The specific authorized information shall be subject to the prompts on the login interface.
(3) You may choose to fill in personal profile information such as nickname, avatar, gender and birthday. Such information helps us provide you with more personalized services, but not filling it in will not affect your normal use of the basic functions of this Service.
(4) To ensure the stable operation of our client and the normal use of related app functions, we will collect your device information (including IMEI, device serial number, OAID, MEID, SIM card serial number), carrier information, de-identified mobile phone number, installed app information, and information on the list of installed apps or running processes.
(5) To identify your device, prevent malicious programs and anti-cheating, improve service security, and ensure operational quality and efficiency, we will collect your device information (including IMEI, device serial number, OAID, MEID, Android ID, IMSI, GUID, MAC address, SIM card serial number, installed app information, or information on running processes).
(6) To provide you with location-based services (including map positioning and recommendations of nearby services), we will call the [Baidu Android Positioning SDK] to collect your device's Android ID for device security verification and service stability assurance. The information collected by this SDK will not be used for other unrelated purposes.
2. Vehicle Binding and Management
(1) When you bind your Dayou Changxing App account to your two-wheeler vehicle, we will collect basic vehicle information such as the frame number (VIN) of your vehicle, vehicle model and vehicle color. This information is collected to determine the correspondence between you and the vehicle, so as to provide you with correct vehicle-related services.
(2) Vehicle usage data: including vehicle lock/unlock status, battery level, estimated remaining range, charging status, Bluetooth connection status, mileage, driving time, energy consumption, OTA update status and other vehicle operation data. Such information is used to help you understand the vehicle status, ensure driving safety, and provide vehicle control functions.
(3) Location information: including the real-time location and historical trajectory of the vehicle. Such information is used for security functions such as vehicle positioning, anti-theft tracking and vehicle recovery. Location information is sensitive personal information. If you do not authorize it, you will be unable to use the above security functions, but you may still use the basic vehicle control functions.
3. Remote Vehicle Control
When you use the remote vehicle control functions, we will record your operation behavior (such as locking/unlocking, flashing lights and sounding the horn, seat heating, etc.) in order to implement these functions and provide you with feedback on the operation results. Such information is necessary to ensure the safe operation of the vehicle.
4. Vehicle Maintenance and Repair Services
(1) Booking maintenance: when you use the maintenance booking function, we will collect your frame number, vehicle name, vehicle model, contact name, contact phone number, booking time, and your location information (used to recommend nearby repair stores).
(2) Fault reporting: when you use the fault reporting function, we will collect your frame number, vehicle name, vehicle model, contact name, contact phone number and problem description. If you upload photos or videos, we will request your authorization for camera and album permissions.
5. Nearby Dealer Search
When you use the function of viewing nearby dealers, we will request your authorization to use your location information in order to recommend the nearest service outlets and calculate distances. Location information is sensitive personal information. If you do not agree to authorize it, you may still manually enter an address to query store information in each city.
6. Community and Interactive Features
(1) When you post updates, articles or comments in the community, we will collect the content you publish (text, images or videos). If you choose to add a geographic location, we will collect that location information.
(2) When you participate in our marketing activities, we may collect information such as your nickname, contact details and delivery address for the purpose of announcing activity results and distributing prizes.
7. Insurance and Value-Added Services
(1) When you purchase insurance services, we will collect your name, ID card number, contact details and vehicle information, and share them with our partner insurance companies in order to provide you with insurance services. The ID card number is sensitive personal information and will be collected only with your explicit consent and only for insurance services.
(2) When you purchase other value-added services, we will collect necessary information such as your order information and payment information.
8. Customer Service and Feedback
When you contact customer service or use the feedback function, we will collect your contact details and the feedback content (text, images, videos). Such information is used to handle your issues and improve service quality. Call recordings are sensitive personal information and will be collected only when you use the telephone customer service.
9. Data Analysis and Product Optimization
We will collect your device information (such as device model, operating system version, application version and network type) and log information (such as IP address, duration of use and operation records) for statistical analysis, improvement of product functions, optimization of user experience and assurance of service security.
(3) Special Statement on Sensitive Personal Information
In the above scenarios, the following information constitutes sensitive personal information, and we will give you special notice:
• Location information: used for vehicle positioning and nearby store search
• Vehicle trajectory information: used for vehicle positioning and anti-theft tracking
We will collect and use your sensitive personal information only after your explicit consent. For sensitive information collection to which you do not consent, we will not provide the corresponding functions, but this will not affect your use of other functions.
(4) Circumstances Where Consent Is Not Required
In accordance with relevant laws and regulations, we may collect and use your personal information without obtaining your consent in the following circumstances:
1. directly related to national security or national defense security;
2. directly related to public security, public health or major public interests;
